# Local paste overhead reference, 2026-09-26

The complete reference run passed semantic/protocol validation across all 18
fixture, formatting and browser combinations. Every observed primary paired
p95 was below the provisional 50 ms objective, ranging from 4.6 to 22.0 ms.
This is one local synthetic-input measurement, not a product-wide latency bound.

The run used the clean Free commit `f9bbee0d036f0fa52c0956b1f630db557b404a31`,
Node 22.23.2 and Playwright 1.58.2 on an Apple M1 Max with 10 logical CPUs and
64 GiB RAM, Darwin 25.5.0 arm64. Headless engines were Chromium 145.0.7632.6,
Firefox 146.0.1 and WebKit 26.0, with the fixed viewport and locale from the
[protocol](../README.md). No other agent-owned build, test or browser suite ran
concurrently. Source review and edits in the separate Pro repository continued.
Initial load averages were 10.98, 18.01 and 27.31; machine idleness, background
process activity and thermal conditions were not independently certified.

All 6,534 blocks completed in 1,408.822 seconds, including first-paste and warmup
blocks. Each table row summarizes 600 measured individual pairs across three
fresh contexts. Negative differences and outliers were retained. There were no
semantic failures, source-hash changes or cleanup failures.

The individual maxima and absolute on/off dispatch distributions are retained in
[the machine-readable summary](./2026-09-26-macos-arm64.json). A passing p95 does
not mean every operation completed below 50 ms. The full local `report.json` and
`samples.jsonl` are under `/private/tmp/domternal-paste-perf-reference-20260926a/`;
their hashes and byte sizes are recorded in that summary. Local scratch artifacts
are not a durable distributed dataset; the protocol and fixed fixtures permit a
new independent run.

Firefox `rich-20k` preserve includes an individual raw paired maximum of 730 ms
and normalized-control maximum of 778 ms; the corresponding series' largest
absolute enabled dispatch was 799 ms. Two of 600 raw pairs and three of 600
normalized-control pairs were at least 50 ms. Their causes were not measured;
the passing p95 does not establish absence of occasional long stalls.

This run covers synchronous vanilla-wrapper synthetic paste, including real
cleanup and default feedback. It excludes native Office clipboard acquisition,
paint latency, asynchronous images, cold module loading, other hardware and the
Pro DOCX converter. Functional behavior in other wrappers is covered separately
by the browser integration matrix. Native clipboard and broader release
qualification remain separate requirements.
